                          LLAMAZAP - The Manual

Written by Jeff Minter & edited with translations by Darryl Still

(The English part of the manual, from the scans at Atarimania. The page
numbers are the manual's own: the game's copy protection asks for a word
by page, paragraph, line and word.)


                                  - 1 -

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS: any Falcon with a COLOUR display (Llamazap will not
run in monochrome), and a new Super Funky joypad.

BACKING UP THE DISK: It is a very good idea to make a copy of the game
disk for day-to-day use and keep the original disk safe in case you spill
a cup of tea on the disk or the dog eats it. Use the FORMAT menu function
to format a new high-density disk and then use the COPY option to copy the
original game disk.

LOADING THE GAME: On a 1M Falcon, or a system without a hard disk drive,
the game must be booted from the floppy disk. Put the disk in the drive and
turn on the Falcon; the game will load and run.

HARD DISK INSTALLATION: If you have a hard disk and more than 1M of
memory, you can install Llamazap on the hard disk and run it from the
Desktop. To do this, create a folder on your hard disk called LLAMAZAP;
drag the STUFF folder from the game disk into this new folder; then open up
the AUTO folder on the game disk and move the LLAMAZAP.PRG icon into the
new folder too. You can then play the game by just opening the Llamazap
folder and double-clicking on LLAMAZAP.PRG.

USE OF RAM: If you have 4M or more, the game will load all the normally
disk-loaded stuff into RAM.

GAME DESCRIPTION: Llamazap is an action game of 25 stages in which you, as
a completely hard and unspeakably beweaponed llama, set out to destroy
invading alien hordes and protect innocent herbivores from molestation.
Usually you fly a spaceship over a bi-directionally scrolling planet, but
frequently you must leave the safety of your ship for tentacle-to-hoof
combat with your enemies. Your aim is to battle your way through to defeat
the last boss on the last level and thereby save the Galaxy and make it a
safer place for ungulates everywhere. Along the way you can earn extra
kudos by rescuing abducted livestock, saving the dolphins, and rescuing
lost and bewildered llamas.

PLAYING THE GAME: Once the game has loaded, you will see a screen
requesting you to type in a particular word from the manual. Type the word
in and press Return, and if you got it right the intro sequence will begin.
If you get it wrong you get thrown back to the Desktop and you'll have to
try again.

The intro sequence displays some text which puts the game into context,
and then displays the title artwork. You can bypass these sequences by
pressing any fire button on the joypad.

After the intro screen you'll get to the main game start screen, where you
have two options: Start the Game and Test-Fly the Ships. Use the joypad to
select one of the options (the selected option has a yak head next to it).
Press a fire button to execute your chosen option.

LEARNING TO FLY: If you have never played Llamazap before, it's a very
good idea to choose the Test-Fly option. This allows you to try out any of
the three spaceships available, and get used to the special weapons, on a
nice peaceful planet with no nasty enemies to hassle you.


                                  - 2 -

The Test-Fly option appears with a 'Select Ship' message, and graphics of
the three ships available, hovering near where your ship will appear. To
select one of the three ships, press any button in a column corresponding
to your ship choice on the keypad.

Each ship is controlled in the same way. Joypad up/down moves the ship in
the corresponding direction. To fire, hold down fire button A or C for
continuous rapid fire. Joypad left/right will accelerate your ship to the
left and right. If you are NOT firing, moving left and right will turn your
ship to face the way you are going. If you keep firing, your ship will stay
pointed in the same direction but you'll still accelerate, so you can, for
example, fly backwards, firing at an enemy advancing towards you whilst
retreating from it. This is a key Llamazap technique which you should
practise in Test-Flight until you're comfortable with it. It's easier to do
than it is to describe, so practise it before you begin the game for real.
You'll need it for the first Mutant Camels sequence.

Fly around a bit, learning about turning and firing. When you are
comfortable with that, try holding down Button B whilst you manoeuvre. The
B button acts as a boost button, doubling your flight speed for as long as
it is held down. Boost is useful to get to that last enemy away on the
other side of the planet, or to outrun some hostile meanie. Take care with
Boost in a crowded situation, though, as it can make you too fast!

USE OF WEAPONS: Each ship has a whole bundle of extra weapons and
power-ups available to increase performance and killing-power. In
Test-Flight mode, all these extras are available for you to experiment
with. If you look at the bottom-right of the screen, you will notice a
matrix of icons. Notice that they are laid out in the same way as the keys
on the keypad. These icons correspond to the keys one to nine on the
keypad; to select a powerup, press the corresponding joypad key.

Many of these powerups are progressive: that is, they can be applied to
your ship more than once. For instance, the main weapon of Ship Two can be
powered up two times, from single bullets to three-way to five-way spread.
If you have reached the limit of such a power-up, it will turn flashing
blue to show it is unavailable. Some options are unavailable with a new
ship: you cannot use the Speed Down function until you have used at least
one Speed Up; you cannot buy an Orb Weapon if you do not have an Orb!

Each ship has a different set of powerup options. Use Test-Flight mode to
try them all out. Practise targets are provided to let you assess the
weaponry. The options, numbered one to nine in the same order as the keypad
buttons, are as follows:

ONE: Change Ships. Selecting this brings back the Select Ship mode,
allowing you to change to a different ship at any time in the game. Select
your new ship using the keypad columns as usual.

TWO: Speed Up. Increases the top speed of your spaceship.

THREE: Speed Down. Decreases your top speed.

FOUR: Increase Main Weapon Power. Makes your main spaceship weapon more
effective.

FIVE: Use Special Weapon. Triggers the special weapon for your ship. Each
ship has a unique and devastating weapon useful in emergencies.

SIX: Auxiliary Weapon. Ships one and two get an Orb with this powerup;
ship three receives an orbiting shield, up to a maximum of four.

SEVEN: Restore Shield. Restores the shield of the current ship to maximum.

EIGHT: Auxiliary Powerup. Ships one and two get firepower for their Orbs;
ship three gets an unarmed Orb.

NINE: Insurance. If you buy Insurance, when you subsequently lose a life,
your new ship will keep all the weapons and goodies you had so
painstakingly accumulated.


                                  - 3 -

USE OF THE ORB: The Orb is indestructible and will try to hover near the
front of your ship. If you hold down the Fire button, The Orb maintains a
static position with respect to your ship. You can manoeuvre to position
The Orb and then hold down FIRE to lock it in place. Using this technique
enables you to hold The Orb out in front of your ship and rub it all over
an enemy (a particularly effective technique against heavily shielded
bosses) or hold it above or below your ship to shield you from attack. The
Orbs of the first two ships can be powered-up with their own weapons for
devastating kill-power.

POWERUP TOKENS: In Test-Flight, all the powerups are available constantly.
In the real game, you have to earn them, and for this you need powerup
tokens. In play, killing some enemies causes them to release a square
token. This is marked with either a camel or an S, and if you pick it up
something wonderful happens. S-tokens trigger a free use of your ship's
Special Weapon. Each Camel token makes available one new Power-Up icon in
the matrix. You can save up to twelve powerup tokens. When you use a
powerup, you effectively 'buy' that powerup for an equivalent number of
stored tokens. If you have twelve tokens saved, you will see 'One-Up'
flashing underneath the matrix; the next token you pick up converts the
last three tokens into an extra life, leaving you with nine stored tokens.

SHIP SHIELD: In Test-Flight the ship is indestructible, so the Shield
Indicator is not shown. In the real game it is displayed at the lower-left
corner of the screen. The shield indicator displays the state of your
shield; if it's green and at the top of the gauge your shields are fine;
if it's red and near the bottom, better use the Restore Shield powerup or
expect to lose a life!

Your shield is damaged by collisions with enemies and their shots.
Obviously, you should try to avoid this.

BONUSES: All the levels of the game contain opportunities to earn special
bonuses for good play. For example, on the Interstellar Space Sheep level,
bonuses are earned by preventing the enemies from mutating your sheep. At
the end of a level, bonuses are tallied and multiplied by a Bonus
Multiplier. The better you played during the level, the higher the Bonus
Multiplier.

LIVES: You begin the game with five lives. Extra lives are obtained during
gameplay by collecting enough Power-Up tokens to get a One-Up.

TRANSITION LEVELS: Every fifth level is designated a Transition Level. For
these levels, llama leaves the ship to do individual combat in a variety of
game challenges. Save up One-Ups so you have plenty of lives for these
levels, as extra lives cannot be obtained during a Transition Level. The
controls for each Transition Level are displayed before the level begins.

WINNING THE GAME: The game is won when you destroy the last boss alien in
Level Five Section Five. You are awarded an incremental bonus for each life
remaining in store when you won, and from then on, you will have the option
to play a more challenging game.

HIGH SCORES: Llamazap maintains a record on disk of the five best scores.
This record is saved to the STUFF folder. If you are running the game from
a floppy disk, ensure that the Write-Protect tab of the disk is off (no
hole) to allow the scores to be written to disk.

PLAYER NAMES AND PROGRESS: When you select the Start Game option, you are
prompted to choose a player name from a table of five names. You can change
any of these names to your own. To do this, move the yak's head to the name
you want to erase and press Option on the keypad. You will be asked to
enter a name; do so and press Return. Your name is now placed on the player
names menu and saved to disk. During play, each time you successfully
complete a Transition Level, one level of progress is credited to your
name. When you subsequently select your name to start a game, you will have
the option to start play directly after the last Transition Level you
completed.


                                  - 4 -

CONVENIENCE CONTROLS: During gameplay the following controls are provided
for your convenience, comfort, and to enable you to go to the toilet
without getting blown up.

PAUSE: pauses the game, oddly enough. While the game is paused, you can
alter the volume of the music by pressing up/down on the joypad. You can
control the volume of the sound effects by holding down the A firebutton
and pressing up/down on the joypad. A sample sound effect is played while
you are adjusting the FX volume. The current volume preferences are saved
to disk along with the high scores.

ESC: If you press ESC on the keyboard during gameplay, the game is aborted
and you are returned to the game start menu. If you press ESC during the
intro sequence or title/menu pages, you will quit the game and return to
the Atari desktop.


                          LLAMAZAP Strategy Guide

The main task in the horizontally-scrolling sections is to figure which are
the key aliens to take out, and then figure out the best way to zap them.
The following level strategies should help you do this.

The best all-round ship for beginners is the ship selected off the
right-hand column on the controller. This ship isn't too frisky, carries a
strong shield and fires penetrative bullets from the outset. However, it is
worth remembering that the ships that are harder to fly yield more points
per enemy shot. For example, shooting an enemy with the flamethrower ship
yields 16x as many points as shooting the same enemy with the beginner's
ship.

ATTACK OF THE MUTANT CAMELS: Position yourself vertically just below the
camels' heads and advance firing shots through the camels' bodies. Ship
Three's penetrative bullets should make meat of the beasts in short order.
Back away still firing when they jump and get close again when they land
again. Skilful players can shoot bullets (especially the shielded one out
of the camel's mouth) to get extra powerup tokens.

INTERSTELLAR SPACE SHEEP: Go straight for the generators on the planet
surfaces. Taking these out prevents any more killer sheep being made, so
you get less hassle during play and a nice fat bonus at the end. When the
sky flashes and you hear a bleat, a new killer has been made, so watch the
scanner for incoming killer sheep.

GET YOUR GOAT: With Ship Three powered up to the three-way spread, you can
take the saucers from the front. Hang in front of the saucer; your
penetrative shots will slice the shielded bullets and zap the saucers.
Watch out for leaping goat-creatures jumping up off the planet. If you have
a ship without penetrative bullets, take the saucers from behind:
projectiles fired that way aren't shielded.

RETURN OF THE MUTANT CAMELS: More camels. Strategy as Level One, except
it's even more important to back off while they are jumping. Use Turbo to
back off fast enough to get out of range of the homers. If you get forced
back against the flame, dodge underneath the camel when it's jumping then
nip underneath. Fly right around the planet to confront the beast as it
emerges from the other side of the flame generators.

LLAMATRONIC: Llamatron. You know the score. If you make your way to the
bottom-left corner of the arena, you can hang there pretty safe just
picking off cows and getting in shots at the generators as they pass by.
Pick up the red bullet powerups: you can kill the generators easily with
these, they're phage bullets that stick to the enemy!

CHASE AND DESTROY: Use Turbo to fly at top speed to the vicinity of the
enemy plane. Come out of Turbo to attack him. Tackle him low on the screen
so the ejecting pilot doesn't go off the top of the screen. If you collect
pilots diligently you should get a few One-Ups on this level. If you're
getting bashed, use Full Shield power-up.


                                  - 5 -

THAT SINKING FEELING: Alternate between picking off ships and rescuing the
dolphins. You get up to eight thousand points for each foiled abduction so
it's well worth doing. If you are using an Orb, you can just hold this
below you and rub it all over the ships to kill them quickly. Otherwise,
just find the right level where you can shoot off all the guns, then hang
there and sink the ship; then move on quickly to the next one without
moving up or down and keep firing. You can finish the level very quickly
this way, but you don't get much dolphin-rescue bonus.

GO, SUB: Try to pick off the submarines when they are high on the screen
as the Nuke pod has further to fall and you have more time to get it. Ship
Three's rotating orbital doobries are useful here to shield you from the
streams of mines the little boats drop on you. Keep an eye on the scanner
and when the boats get near, go deep: a lot of mines explode before they
reach bottom. A good player can get more One-Ups here.

GIANT MUTANT FISH FROM HELL: Get the jellyfish as fast as you can: this
level is completely underwater, so you can't outrun Baiters. If you have
orbitals, watch out for triggering Electric Eels accidentally: they lunge
for you and take several shots to kill. You can outrun them if you use
Turbo. You can get a lot of powerups and One-Ups if you rescue the dolphins
instead of killing them: if you approach them carefully they will stop
bombing and allow you to collect them.

METAGALACTIC: Metagalactic Llamas level. Have your finger on Fire B so you
can jump if necessary. The edge of the screen is a good place to be, keep
the force field high and bounce your shots off the edge and back around to
cover the entire screen. When spiders land, bring the force field low to
bounce shots down onto the ground and zap them. Use the screen wrap: if
something is getting too close wander off screen, bring down the
forcefield and zap him from the other side. You can get an increasing bonus
here by shooting spiders BEFORE they land on the ground. Protect your
goats!

ASTEROIDS: Blast the rocks and mop up strays promptly so you're not around
when the Baiters come. The little saucers are more of a nuisance than a
serious threat; pick them off to get a higher bonus at the end of the
level. Use Turbo to chase down the last few strays. Watch out for the
arrowhead ships; getting caught at their convergence is not good for your
health. I usually change to Ship Two on this level, with the main weapon
powered up to three-way and the faster speed it's better at mopping up
stray asteroids, and it's useful on later levels too.

YETANOTHERGAMESTYLE: Quite straightforward; pause in your firing at the
start of each wave of attackers as there's often a floating llama to
collect. An Orb held below you when the rockets come up from the bottom is
useful. Learn the best ways to get all the llamas: if you miss them it
damages your bonus. Use the Orb to kill the final boss.

MERRILY WE SCROLL ALONG: Learn where you can break the surface and get at
the lost llamas to get lots of One-Ups during this level. The gigantic
bouncing camel can't be killed: you have to go underneath at the right
time. You can destroy the boss quickly by rubbing an orb on the ends of his
tentacles. There is a very excellent cheat on this level which costs you
one powerup but gets you past a lot of dangerous stuff!

ASTEROIDS II: Like Asteroids, but the rocks rush you as they fragment.
Crack a big one then back off firing to mop up the fragments, but mind you
don't back into another one coming at you from behind!

GRIDRUNNER: Listen to the rhythm of the Zapper and learn to be aware of
where the next bolt will be, and not be there! The little bolt from the
side doesn't harm you. You get a big end-of-level bonus here by NOT
shooting the little goats that run across the Grid from time to time, but
don't hang too close to the side when you destroy a snake because a goat
may well emerge and hit you before you can get out the way. You also get a
nice cumulative bonus for not killing goats, but it's reset if you kill
one.


                                  - 6 -

ATOMIC: You can't kill the big atoms straight away. Look for the molecule
with an orbiting companion, zap it and grab the little molecule with your
ship. Take it to the nearest big atom and it'll break up, emitting
particles and a nucleus which chases you. Line up with the enraged nucleus
and back off using Turbo whilst letting him have it with your guns.

INSECTS: Kill the first hive and dragonfly, then take out the spiders as a
matter of priority. Wait until they drop off their webs and zap them on the
ground, unless they are taking one of your sheep. For subsequent
dragonflies, back away from them firing, and manoeuvre vertically so as to
damp their oscillation so you can hold them in a continuous beam of fire.
Beware of the spiders' webs, if you fly through one, you're history! If you
find the right place in this level you can hunt spiders for a big bonus,
but watch out because they can appear right above you...

BRAINS: Get in as many shots as you can, then when the rotating eyeballs
come for you, use Turbo to dodge to the other side of the brain then turn
around for a clear shot at the naked brain. Zap them low if possible as it
gives you more time to collect the lightbulb.

DEATH TO CUTE: Get used to the periodic oscillation of each box and hang
just outside its range spraying bullets. When the box opens and meanies
come out, back off. Beware especially the propeller-bombs the big black
bombs' shrapnel.

CAMELID SHOWDOWN: Hover high in the corner, then when the giant flying
camel gets close, drop down, zip across to the opposite corner and wait for
him there. A few iterations of this should suffice. Watch out for the
devious shots which can swing around and catch you unawares!

SON OF LLAMATRON: The cow-generators move more intelligently in this level
so the old left-corner trick no longer works. Avoid the generators and
shoot cows until you get a red-bullets icon. These bullets are penetrative
and stick to the enemies, a good burst will kill a generator very quickly.

RETURN TO THE EDGE OF TIME: Similar tactics to first time around, but
watch out for opening a knife bubble directly overhead: the plummeting
knife cannot be killed. Keep the forcefield not quite at the top most of
the time; if a floating fork emerges you can flick the forcefield high to
get it before it drops. Zap the bovver boots quickly or they'll stomp on
your goats. Shoot the pieces of the fragmenting diamond things before they
hit the ground for a big bonus.

YOU THOUGHT IT WAS HARD BEFORE: The intense Gridrunner experience. The
Zapper goes more frequently. Watch out for the little spheres, which will
fragment of their own accord even if you don't shoot them. Best to get
underneath them and open them yourself. Concentrate on killing snakes; the
faster you get rid of them the sooner the wave will end. Basically just
stay cool and hang in there and you'll get through.

LLAMA IN A JETPAC: I usually take out this boss's guns in the following
order: bottom-left, top-left, top-right then bottom-right. You can hang at
the top of the screen; it's safe up there. The large shots can be shot, so
don't panic if you get caught low while tackling that last gun.

FINAL FIGHT: Your bullets are used as reaction-mass to move you, so if you
fire one way you move the other. If you hold down Fire, the joypad moves
the llama and the bullets come out the opposite way. I find this a lot
easier to grok. Learn where each enemy emerges and position yourself
accordingly. Bounce shots off the screen edges. The final boss emerges
after the shower of yaks' heads. He emits atoms at you, kill him and you
get a congratulatory message saying what a hard llama you are and how
females of 18 different species are requesting your hoof in marriage, then
the game ends, and Master Mode is activated.


                                  - 7 -

                               SPECIAL TIPS

If you are about to lose your last life, remember that you have two other
ships, possibly unused and each with a full shield. Use the Change Ships
icon to switch to another ship before you die.

When you are doing well, take time out to buy Insurance. You can carry on
collecting more power and weapons, comfortable in the knowledge that you'll
keep them all in the afterlife.

In general, once proficient at the scrolling wave a good player will get
Plenty of lives in hand to use in the final five levels; those lives will
be needed, it's hard!
